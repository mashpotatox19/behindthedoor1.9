        Before you pay or sign

        1. See the inside in person, or on a live video call where they walk you through it.
        2. Call the owner or leasing office on a number you found yourself, and ask if this listing is theirs. Find the official website
        3. Pay through a lease portal, check or card. Never by wire, gift card, crypto, or Zelle to someone you have not met.
        4. Read the whole lease, get every promise in writing, and take dated photos when you move in.
